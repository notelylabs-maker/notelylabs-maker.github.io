Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Summary

Notely AI does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your notes stay on your iPad unless you choose to back them up to your own iCloud Drive. If you turn on Smart Tutor, the questions you ask Tutor are sent to an AI service to be answered, as explained below; nothing is kept about you.

Your notes

Everything you write, draw, import or photograph in Notely AI is stored on your iPad, inside Notely AI's private storage. The developer never receives, sees or stores your notes.

Handwriting recognition

Search, Solve Math, Handwriting to Text and Text from Photos read your handwriting using Apple's on-device Vision technology. This happens entirely on your iPad; nothing is uploaded.

iCloud Drive backups

If you turn on iCloud Backup, Notely AI saves backup files to a folder you choose in your own iCloud Drive. They are stored by Apple under your Apple ID and are governed by Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot access them. You can turn backups off and delete the files at any time in the Files app.

Notely Tutor

Without Smart Tutor, Notely Tutor runs entirely on your iPad, using Apple Intelligence where available, or a free AI model you can choose to download (a Qwen model by Alibaba Cloud, Apache License 2.0). Downloading the model fetches only the model file from Hugging Face. If you turn on Look Things Up, the key words of a question you ask Tutor are searched on Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation) to find facts; your notes are never sent. It's off unless you turn it on.

Smart Tutor (optional)

Smart Tutor is off until you agree to turn it on, and you can turn it off any time in Settings › Notely Tutor. When it's on and you ask Tutor something (or, with Notely Plus, when Coach checks what you just wrote), Notely AI sends that request to the developer's server (run on Cloudflare), which passes it to Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, and returns the answer. A request contains your question, the part of your notes Tutor needs to answer it, your answer-length setting, a random ID created by the app (not linked to you, your Apple ID or your iPad's hardware) and, if you have Notely Plus, Apple's signed record of your subscription (it contains no name or email). Your IP address is seen by the server as with any internet connection. The developer's server does not store or log your questions or answers; it keeps only daily counters, named by one-way scrambled (hashed) versions of the random ID and IP address, to limit how many answers each device gets, and deletes them after 2 days. Anthropic processes requests to provide the answer, does not use them to train its AI, and may keep them for up to 30 days for safety and abuse monitoring under its own privacy policy and commercial terms. Cloudflare processes the connection under its privacy policy. Nothing is used for advertising or tracking, and nothing is sold.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are only used when you choose to add a photo or scan a page. Images are saved inside the note you added them to.

Face ID / Touch ID

When you lock a note, iPadOS checks your Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. Notely AI only receives a yes or no answer; it never has access to your biometric data.

Purchases

Notely AI and Notely Plus are sold through the App Store. Apple processes all payments. Notely AI only learns whether you have an active subscription; it never sees your name, email or payment details.

Children

Notely AI is designed for students. It has no accounts and stores nothing about anyone, including children. Smart Tutor sends only the question being asked, as described above, and only after someone turns it on.

Deleting your data

Because Notely AI has no account and the Smart Tutor server keeps no questions or answers, there is nothing stored about you anywhere else. To stop sending anything, turn off Smart Tutor. Deleting a note (and emptying Trash) or deleting the app removes your notes from your iPad. Delete any iCloud backups in the Files app.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published in the app and on the support website before it takes effect.
