Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Summary

Notely AI does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your notes stay on your iPad unless you choose to back them up to your own iCloud Drive.

Your notes

Everything you write, draw, import or photograph in Notely AI is stored on your iPad, inside Notely AI's private storage. The developer never receives, sees or stores your notes.

Handwriting recognition

Search, Solve Math, Handwriting to Text and Text from Photos read your handwriting using Apple's on-device Vision technology. This happens entirely on your iPad; nothing is uploaded.

iCloud Drive backups

If you turn on iCloud Backup, Notely AI saves backup files to a folder you choose in your own iCloud Drive. They are stored by Apple under your Apple ID and are governed by Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot access them. You can turn backups off and delete the files at any time in the Files app.

Notely Tutor

Notely Tutor runs entirely on your iPad, using Apple Intelligence where available, or a free AI model you can choose to download (a Qwen model by Alibaba Cloud, Apache License 2.0). Downloading the model fetches only the model file from Hugging Face. If you turn on Look Things Up, the key words of a question you ask Tutor are searched on Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation) to find facts; your notes are never sent. It's off unless you turn it on.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are only used when you choose to add a photo or scan a page. Images are saved inside the note you added them to.

Face ID / Touch ID

When you lock a note, iPadOS checks your Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. Notely AI only receives a yes or no answer; it never has access to your biometric data.

Purchases

Notely AI and Notely Plus are sold through the App Store. Apple processes all payments. Notely AI only learns whether you have an active subscription; it never sees your name, email or payment details.

Children

Notely AI collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Because Notely AI has no account and no server, there is nothing stored about you anywhere else. Deleting a note (and emptying Trash) or deleting the app removes your notes from your iPad. Delete any iCloud backups in the Files app.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published in the app and on the support website before it takes effect.
