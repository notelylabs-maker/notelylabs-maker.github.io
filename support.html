Where are my notes saved?

On your iPad, saved automatically as you write. Turn on Settings › Backup & Restore › iCloud Backup to keep copies in your iCloud Drive.

I deleted a note by mistake

Open Library › Trash. Deleted notes stay there for 30 days. Inside a note, ⋯ › Version History brings back earlier versions.

Moving to a new iPad

Install Notely AI, open Settings › Backup & Restore › Restore from a Backup File, and choose your backup in iCloud Drive.

Using Notely Tutor

Open a note and tap Tutor. Type a question, or write one on your page ending with “?” and pause. Tap Page to answer every question on your page, or Test for a practice test. The mode button switches between Quick, Step by step, Practice test and Flashcards.

Tutor needs its AI

On iPads with Apple Intelligence, Tutor uses it automatically. On other iPads, tap Download Tutor AI in the Tutor panel once (Wi-Fi recommended); after that it works offline. Math is solved exactly on every iPad, even without the AI.

Tutor gave a wrong answer

Tutor's answers come from an AI running on your iPad and can be wrong, especially on older iPads with the smaller model. Check important answers. Clear handwriting and a question mark at the end help it read your question.

Notely Plus

Plus gives you unlimited Notely Tutor (without it: 5 messages a day) and unlocks Solve Math, Handwriting to Text, Copy as Text and Text from Photos. Restore it on a new device with Settings › Notely Plus › Restore Purchases. Cancel any time in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

My palm leaves marks

Turn on Apple Pencil Only (the pencil button in a note's top bar, or Settings › Writing).
