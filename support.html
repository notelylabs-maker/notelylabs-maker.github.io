Notely Plus and your Apple ID

Do I need a Notely account?

No. There is no sign-up, login or password in Notely AI. Your Apple ID, the one already on your iPad, is all you need.

Notely Plus is bought through Apple and belongs to that Apple ID. We never see your name, email or payment details.

How do I get Plus on another iPad or iPhone?
  1. Sign in to the device with the same Apple ID you bought Plus with (Settings › your name).
  2. Download Notely AI from the App Store.
  3. Open it. Plus turns on by itself.

If it doesn’t, open Settings › Notely Plus › Restore Purchases in the app. You are never charged twice.

Plus isn’t showing up

Check that the device uses the same Apple ID that paid for Plus. A purchase belongs to the Apple ID that made it, so a different Apple ID won’t see it.

Make sure you’re online, then tap Settings › Notely Plus › Restore Purchases. If it still doesn’t appear, email us.

How do I cancel?

On your iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Notely Plus › Cancel Subscription. You keep Plus until the end of the time you’ve already paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can’t issue refunds ourselves.

What happens if Plus ends?

Nothing you wrote is lost or locked. Text you already converted stays in your notes. Tutor goes back to 5 messages a day, and Solve Math, Handwriting to Text, Copy as Text and Text from Photos lock until you subscribe again.

Moving to a new iPad

Your subscription moves by itself. Your notes need one step.

Your notes are stored on the iPad itself (there is no Notely server), so they move with a backup file:

  1. On the old iPad: Notely › Settings › Backup & Restore › Turn On iCloud Backup.
  2. On the new iPad, signed in to the same Apple ID: install Notely AI, open Settings › Backup & Restore › Restore from a Backup File, and choose your backup in iCloud Drive.

Every note, PDF, image, subject, flashcard and setting comes back.

Common mix-ups

“Deleting the app cancels my subscription.”

It doesn’t. A subscription keeps renewing until you cancel it in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

“Deleting and reinstalling Notely is a safe way to fix something.”

Deleting the app deletes the notes stored on that iPad. Back up first (Settings › Backup & Restore), or just email us. Updating the app never touches your notes.

“My notes are in the cloud automatically.”

Not unless you turn on iCloud Backup. Then Notely saves a copy of your whole library to your iCloud Drive when you leave a note (at most every 10 minutes).

“My notes sync live between my iPad and iPhone.”

They don’t sync live. Each device keeps its own notes. Move them with a backup file, as above.

“I have to pay again on each device.”

No. Plus works on every iPad and iPhone signed in to the Apple ID that bought it.

“The 5 free Tutor messages are all I get.”

It’s 5 a day. They come back every day at midnight, your time. Plus gives you 100 a day.

“Tutor sends my notes to an AI company.”

It doesn’t. Tutor runs on your iPad, with Apple Intelligence or a free model you download once. Your notes never leave the device. The optional Look Things Up setting (off unless you turn it on) searches only the key words of a question on Wikipedia.

“Tutor needs internet.”

Only for the one-time AI download on iPads without Apple Intelligence, and for Look Things Up. After that it works offline.

“Tutor is always right.”

Tutor is an AI and can be wrong, especially on older iPads that use the smaller model. Math is solved exactly, but check other important answers.

Your notes

Where are my notes saved?

On your iPad, saved automatically as you write. Notely also keeps a daily safety backup on the iPad, and a backup before every update. Turn on iCloud Backup to keep copies outside the iPad too.

I deleted a note by mistake

Open Library › Trash. Deleted notes stay there for 30 days. Inside a note, ⋯ › Version History brings back earlier versions.

My palm leaves marks

Turn on Apple Pencil Only (the pencil button in a note’s top bar, or Settings › Writing).

Notely Tutor

Using Notely Tutor

Open a note and tap Tutor. Type a question, or write one on your page ending with “?” and pause. Tap Page to answer every question on your page, or Test for a practice test. The mode button switches between Quick, Step by step, Practice test and Flashcards.

Tutor needs its AI

On iPads with Apple Intelligence, Tutor uses it automatically. On other iPads, tap Download Tutor AI in the Tutor panel once (Wi-Fi recommended). Math is solved exactly on every iPad, even without the AI.

Tutor gave a wrong answer

Tutor’s answers come from an AI running on your iPad and can be wrong. Check important answers. Clear handwriting and a question mark at the end help it read your question.
